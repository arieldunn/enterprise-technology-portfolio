Case study 01Healthcare software

Connecting customer operations across a fragmented technology ecosystem

01

A growing healthcare software organization managed 40,000+ annual support tickets across disconnected sales, marketing, support, documentation, engineering, and success platforms.

Focus

Enterprise platforms · Customer experience · Support technology · AI · Integrations

User / business problem

How should customer information, knowledge, workflows, ownership, automation, and support operate across the entire customer lifecycle?

My role

Led stakeholder discovery and shaped requirements across CRM and data architecture, lifecycle design, support workflows, SLAs, engineering escalations, knowledge architecture, self-service, reporting, training, and adoption.

Product / technology decisions

Prioritized a shared customer model, governed knowledge, automation, and AI-assisted support while preserving human review for complex decisions and escalation paths.

Cross-functional work

Aligned sales, marketing, support, customer success, documentation, engineering, and leadership around shared workflows, ownership, and measures of success.

Outcome

A centralized customer environment, stronger cross-functional visibility, scalable support operations, modernized self-service, improved reporting, and a foundation for continued AI adoption.

What this demonstrates

Enterprise product thinking · AI enablement · Knowledge systems · Cross-functional platform strategy · Systems integration · Adoption

Case study 02Equipment rental & sales

Designing enterprise technology around the way people actually work

02

A multi-location organization had a CRM, but it did not reflect how sales teams worked across customers, equipment opportunities, projects, territories, and operational systems.

Focus

Product design · Custom integrations · Data modeling · Mobile UX · Adoption

User / business problem

Field teams needed both a customer view and project-level context without forcing their work into a generic, desk-first CRM model.

My role

Led stakeholder discovery and translated field workflows into requirements for the data model, territory logic, ownership, integrations, reporting, mobile experience, training, and enablement.

Product / technology decisions

Connected accounts, opportunities, equipment, and large construction projects; prioritized mobile usability; and balanced standardized information with the realities of work between customers and jobsites.

Cross-functional work

Worked across field representatives, sales and operations leaders, administrators, and technical partners to test workflows and resolve integration tradeoffs.

Outcome

Clearer workflows, connected operational data, stronger project visibility, consistent information, improved reporting, and a scalable platform for growth.

Case study 03Healthcare consulting

Scaling an enterprise technology ecosystem through growth and acquisition

03

A national consulting organization needed its technology ecosystem to support hundreds of users, multiple business units, acquired organizations, CRM and ERP data, governance, AI, and future enterprise applications.

Focus

Enterprise platforms · ERP integration · M&A · Data governance · AI · LMS evaluation

User / business problem

Teams needed one dependable enterprise environment without erasing the different workflows of business units and newly acquired organizations.

My role

Connected business requirements, users, platform capabilities, data, integrations, and the long-term roadmap as a strategic resource and primary point of contact.

Product / technology decisions

Balanced common standards with team flexibility across role-based access, pipelines, custom data models, lifecycle automation, reporting, adoption measurement, and CRM/ERP integration.

Cross-functional work

Aligned executives, business-unit leaders, end users, technical teams, vendors, and acquired organizations through discovery, prioritization, testing, migration, and enablement.

Data & governance

Developed a reusable acquisition framework—Discovery → Inventory → Mapping → Cleansing → Test → Validate → Migrate → Enable—and helped reduce roughly 200 duplicate company records to zero.

Outcome

Enterprise-wide adoption, 200+ users trained and enabled, repeatable acquisition integration, CRM/ERP connectivity, governed data, scalable automation, AI enablement, and a long-term modernization roadmap.

Technology evaluation

Evaluated enterprise technology options based on business fit, integration requirements, APIs and webhooks, workflow impact, long-term maintainability, and user needs.

How I think

Product starts with understanding the user.

A technically correct system nobody wants to use is not a successful product.

I start with the person doing the work, what they need, where decisions happen, and where handoffs break—then work backward into the technology.

  1. 01Where does the data come from—and who owns it?
  2. 02What does the user actually experience?
  3. 03What happens when the ideal workflow fails?
  4. 04What will this cost to operate and maintain?
  5. 05What will success look like six months after launch?

AI & emerging technology

AI becomes valuable when it fits the work.

I approach AI as operational capability, not novelty: evaluate where it should—and should not—be used, prepare trusted knowledge and permissions, connect it to real workflows, define human review, and measure whether people adopt it responsibly.
